# U. G. Semester - II Examination 2024-25 (NEP)
## Sociology
### Paper: SOCMD21 : Indian Knowledge System
**Time:** 2:30 Hours  
**Full Marks:** 70  

---

### **Instructions:**
The question paper is divided into three Sections:
- **Section - A:** Objective type questions of 2 marks each, maximum 50 words.
- **Section - B:** Short answer type questions of 250 words each. Each of 10 marks. Attempt any three questions.
- **Section - C:** Long answer type questions each of 15 marks. Attempt any two questions.

---

### **SECTION - A**
**(Objective Type Questions)**  
*(5 × 2 = 10 Marks | Maximum 50 words each)*

1. **Answer the following:**
   - (a) Who is the author of the book 'Nationalist Thought and the Colonial World : A Derivative Discourse'?
   - (b) Distinguish between art and imitation.
   - (c) Who is the ideal artist according to Sri Aurobindo?
   - (d) What do you mean by 'Trusteeship'?
   - (e) Who is known as the father of modern aesthetics?
