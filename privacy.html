Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 9, 2026

security 1. Overview & Commitment

Welcome to SCIQB. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. Because our platform is designed primarily as a localized academic preparation workstation, we operate under a strict minimalist data collection model. This Privacy Policy details how we manage and secure the information relating to your mock examinations and test histories.

database 2. Data Collection & Local Storage

Unlike standard web portals, we do not collect, harvest, or transmit your personal data to centralized external servers. All session progress, answers selected, test attempts, scores, and analytics are stored locally on your browser using standard local storage technologies (such as Web Storage or IndexedDB).

  • No User Accounts: You do not need to register or sign in to use SCIQB.
  • Offline Control: Since your scores and test attempts reside locally on your device, clearing your browser cache/cookies will reset your mock exam statistics.
  • Image Storage: While the platform supports uploading mock examination answer sheets for proctored mode evaluations, persistent cloud-based image storage is disabled. Uploaded answers are processed solely for the duration of the current evaluation session.

psychology 3. AI Evaluation & Processing

Our platform integrates AI-based grading to deliver instant feedback on solution sheets. When you submit a theory response or image for evaluation:

  • The context or image content is temporarily processed by the AI logical evaluation model.
  • This processing is transient, and no personal identifying markers are attached.
  • The AI model uses inputs purely for comparative analysis against official department answer keys and academic metrics.

info 4. Third-Party Integrations & Assets

SCIQB loads specific assets from verified third-party Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) for styling, fonts, and math formulas rendering (such as Tailwind CSS, Google Fonts, Material Symbols, and MathJax). These third-party CDNs may automatically collect metadata like your IP address to deliver the resources efficiently.

mail 5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or how your local exam logs are handled, feel free to contact us through the repository channels or academic workstation contacts.
