\noindent   \textbf{Question 4.}
Make a précis of the following passage and suggest a suitable title:\pts{10}

``On one occasion in Russia, after a battle, a famous surgeon wanted to attend to the wounds of a general first. Napoleon groaned: `Your duty', he said to the surgeon, `is to attend to all the wounded and not to anyone in particular'. In Russia, when the victorious hoofs of a horse were about to go over the dying body of a Russian soldier, one of Napoleon's generals said, `Oh! This is only a Russian soldier'. Napoleon lifted up the body of the dying man. He said: `After victory we have no Russians or Frenchmen. They are all men'.''
