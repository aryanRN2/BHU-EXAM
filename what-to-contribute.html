What Can You Contribute to SCIQB?

Help build the largest open academic archive for Banaras Hindu University. Share question papers, notes, or code to earn contribution points and appear on the Leaderboard!

description

Question Papers (PYQs)

50 Points

Photos or PDF scans of Mid-Term, End-Term, and Sessional question papers for any BHU course (NEP or Old syllabus).

  • check_circleClear, readable page photos
  • check_circleMentions Subject & Year
menu_book

Handwritten / Digital Notes

250 Points

Unit-wise lecture notes, summary guides, solved numericals, or detailed topic explanations for BHU subjects.

  • check_circleUnit-wise structured notes
  • check_circleClean PDF format
code

LaTeX Transcriptions

1 Pt / Line

Transcribe raw paper images into clean `.tex` source code using our standardized LaTeX template.

  • check_circleUses standard LaTeX template
  • check_circleValidates compiling PDF

Slides & Presentations

5 Pts / Page

Classroom PPT slides, seminar decks, or faculty lecture presentations.

  • check_circlePPTX or PDF decks
  • check_circleSubject syllabus aligned

Practical Files & Lab Manuals

100 -- 300 Points

Practical experiment record files, lab manuals, SageMath/Python code notebooks, or chemistry lab procedure writeups.

  • check_circleCovers experiments with data/calculations
  • check_circlePhysics, Chemistry, Computer Science, Botany, Zoology labs

Submit Resources in 2 Steps

Ready to Submit & Earn Points?

  1. Take clear photos or convert your study material into a readable PDF document.
  2. Send your file directly to our Telegram community or developer team with your name & course details.
  3. Our audit team verifies the submission and awards your contribution points on the live Leaderboard!
