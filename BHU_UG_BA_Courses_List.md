# Banaras Hindu University (BHU) - UG BA Courses & Subject List

This document provides a complete guide and structured list of undergraduate courses offered under **B.A. (Hons.)** at Banaras Hindu University (BHU), including missing subjects added from official UG bulletins.

---

## 1. Overview & Degree Classification

In BHU Undergraduate admissions (CUET-UG):
* **B.A. (Hons.) Arts & B.A. (Hons.) Social Sciences** are the primary Bachelor of Arts degree programs.
* **B.F.A. (Bachelor of Fine Arts)** and **B.P.A. (Bachelor of Performing Arts)** are professional 4-year degree programs separate from B.A. and require practical entrance tests.
* **Academic & Research Centres** (e.g., Bharat Adhyayan Kendra, Peace Research) offer minor/elective/diploma courses rather than standalone main B.A. Honours degrees.
