\noindent\textbf{Question 1} \textit{Attempt any four of the following:}\pts{$4 \times 3.5 = 14$}
\begin{parts}
  \item Show that in a perfectly inelastic collision in a laboratory system, there is always a loss of kinetic energy.
  \item What couple must be applied to a one-meter-long wire, having $1\text{ mm}$ diameter, in order to twist one end of it through 90 degrees, while the other end is fixed? Rigidity of the material of the wire is $\eta = 2.8 \times 10^{10}\text{ N/m}^2$.
  \item Explain inertial and non-inertial frames of reference with examples.
  \item A uniform circular ring of mass $M$ and radius $R$ hangs in a vertical plane supported by a knife edge at one point on its inner circumference. Calculate the natural frequency of small oscillation.
  \item If the centre of mass of three particles of masses $1\text{ kg}$, $2\text{ kg}$, and $3\text{ kg}$ is at the point $(3, 3, 3)$, where should a fourth mass of $4\text{ kg}$ be placed so that the centre of mass of the four particles is at the point $(1, 1, 1)$?
  \item Given that the orbital velocity of the Sun about the centre of our galaxy is $3 \times 10^7\text{ cm/s}$ and its distance is nearly equal to $3 \times 10^{22}\text{ cm}$ from the axis of the galaxy, estimate the mass of the galaxy ($G = 6.67 \times 10^{-8}\text{ CGS units}$).
\end{parts}

\noindent\textbf{Question 2}
\begin{parts}
  \item Derive an expression for twisting couple per unit twist for a solid cylinder. Show that for cylinders of the same mass, material and length, twisting couple per unit twist is greater for a hollow cylinder than for a solid one.\pts{7}
  \item Derive the relation connecting Young's modulus ($Y$), Bulk modulus ($K$) and Shear modulus ($\eta$) of a material.\pts{7}
\end{parts}

\noindent\textbf{Question 3}\pts{14} \\
Write down the differential equation of a damped harmonic oscillator and solve it. Use the solution to explain three different conditions of damping.

\begin{center}\textbf{OR}\end{center}

\begin{parts}
  \item The frequency of a damped harmonic oscillator is equal to half the frequency experienced by the oscillator without damping. Calculate the logarithmic decrement of the system.
  \item A harmonic oscillator consisting of a $50\text{ g}$ mass attached to a massless spring has quality factor $200$. If it oscillates with an amplitude of $2\text{ cm}$ in resonance with a periodic force of frequency $20\text{ cycles/sec}$, calculate:
  \begin{parts}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the average energy stored in it,
    \item the rate of dissipation of energy.
  \end{parts}
\end{parts}

  \item A horizontal pipe of a non-uniform bore has water flowing through it such that the velocity of flow is $40\text{ cm/s}$ at a point where the pressure is $2\text{ cm}$ of mercury column. What is the pressure at a point where the velocity of flow is $60\text{ cm/s}$? (Take $g = 980\text{ cm/s}^2$ and density of water $= 1\text{ g/cm}^3$).\pts{4}
