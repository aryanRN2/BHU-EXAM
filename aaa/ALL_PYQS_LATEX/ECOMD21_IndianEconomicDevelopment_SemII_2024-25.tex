    \end{parts}

\centerline{   \textbf{SECTION B}}

\noindent   \textit{Note: Write your answer in about 250 words. Each question carries 5 marks.\hfill [$4  \times 5 = 20$ Marks]}


\begin{enumerate}[label=   \textbf{\arabic*.}]
    \setcounter{enumi}{1}
    \item What was the main objective of India's First Five-Year Plan?\pts{5}
    
\begin{center}   \textbf{OR}\end{center}
    Discuss the implications of a service-led growth model for inclusive development and employment in India.\pts{5}

    \item What were the main causes of the 1991 Balance of Payments crisis in India? Explain how it led to a shift in economic policy.\pts{5}
    
\begin{center}   \textbf{OR}\end{center}
    Evaluate the performance of Indian agriculture in terms of productivity and growth during the Green Revolution period.\pts{5}

    \item What are the major criticisms of the Planning Commission that led to the formation of NITI Aayog?\pts{5}
    
\begin{center}   \textbf{OR}\end{center}
    Despite high growth in the services sector, employment generation remains a concern. Critically analyse this paradox.\pts{5}

    \item Critically examine the effectiveness of the Public Distribution System in ensuring food security in India.\pts{5}
    
\begin{center}   \textbf{OR}\end{center}
    Discuss the structural distortions in the Indian economy caused by British colonial rule. How did they affect India's post-independence planning priorities?\pts{5}
