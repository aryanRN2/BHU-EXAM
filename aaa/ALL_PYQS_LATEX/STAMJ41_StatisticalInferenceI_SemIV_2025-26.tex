\noindent   \textbf{Q1.}   \textit{(Compulsory)}

\begin{parts}
  \item Discuss consistency criterion of a good estimator. If $X_1, X_2, \dots, X_n$ are $n$ random observations taken from geometric distribution with parameter $ \theta$, then show that $ar{X}$ is consistent estimator of $\frac{1}{ \theta}$. Also, obtain a consistent estimator of $e^{1/ \theta}$.

  \item Explain point and interval estimation with proper examples. Write the confidence interval for the population mean when variance is known.

  \item Discuss sampling distribution of a statistic and standard error with its utility. A machine produces a large number of items of which 15\% are found to be defective. If a random sample of 200 items is taken from the population, then find the standard error of sampling distribution of proportion.

  \item Write difference between parametric and non-parametric tests. The cars that enter in a parking are classified either Foreign-made (   \textbf{F}) or Indian-Made (   \textbf{I}). To check that the car enter in the parking are in random order, a sample of first 40 cars that entered in the parking is taken and found the following sequence:
  
\begin{center}
   \texttt{IIFFFIFFFFFIIFIIIFFIFFIFFFIFFIIFFFIFFII}
  \end{center}
  Obtain number of runs and expected value of number of runs in the above sequence?
\end{parts}

\noindent   \textbf{Q2.}

\begin{parts}
  \item Define Efficiency. If $X_1, X_2, \dots, X_n$ is a random sample taken from a population having mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$, then show that the statistic $T' = \frac{1}{n+1}\sum_{i=1}^n X_i$ is biased but more efficient than sample mean for estimating the population mean.

  \item If $T_1$ and $T_2$ be the two unbiased estimators of $\gamma( \theta)$ with variances $\sigma_1^2$ and $\sigma_2^2$ and correlation coefficient $\rho$, what is the best linear unbiased combination of $T_1$ and $T_2$, and what is the variance of such a combination?
\end{parts}

\noindent   \textbf{Q3.}

\begin{parts}
  \item Explain the following with suitable example:
  
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
    \item Simple and composite hypothesis
    \item Types of errors in hypothesis testing
    \item Critical region and P-value
    \item Power of a test

\noindent   \textbf{Q4.}

\begin{parts}
  \item Twenty people were attacked by a disease and only 18 survived. Will you reject the hypothesis that the survival rate, if attacked by this disease, is $85\%$ in favour of the hypothesis that it is more, at $5\%$ level of significance? (You may use large sample test) \\
  {[Given that $\sqrt{0.006375} = 0.0798$, and $Z_{0.05} = 1.645$]}

  \item Describe Chi-square test of independence of attributes and its assumptions. Also, show that:
  \[ \chi^2 = \left[ \sum_{i=1}^n \left(\frac{O_i^2}{E_i}\right) \right] - N \]
  where notations are having their usual meaning.
\end{parts}

\noindent   \textbf{Q5.} What is difference between dependent and independent sample t-tests? Write assumptions for t-test to make inferences about significance difference between two population means. A drug is given to 8 patients and the increments in their blood pressure are recorded to be $4, 0, 7, -2, 0, -3, 2, 0$. Assume that increment in their blood pressure follows normal distribution. Is it reasonable to believe that the drug has no effect on the change of blood pressure at $5\%$ level of significance? \\
{[Given that $\sqrt{10.57} = 3.25$, $\sqrt{2} = 1.4142$ and $t_{7, 0.025} = 2.365$]}
