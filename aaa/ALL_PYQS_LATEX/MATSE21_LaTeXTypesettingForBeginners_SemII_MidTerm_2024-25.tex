\noindent \textbf{Question 4.} Write a \LaTeX{} code to produce the following output: \pts{5}
\[
\begin{aligned}
E[|X|] &= \int_{x} |x| f_X dx \\
&= \int_{|x| \ge a} |x| f_X dx + \int_{|x| < a} |x| f_X dx \\
&\ge \int_{|x| \ge a} |x| f_X dx \\
&\ge a \int_{|x| \ge a} f_X dx \\
&= a E[|X| \ge a] \\
\therefore E[|X| \ge a] &\le \frac{E[|X|]}{a}.
\end{aligned}
\]
