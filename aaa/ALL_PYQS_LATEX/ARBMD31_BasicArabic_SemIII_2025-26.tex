\noindent\textbf{Question 1.} Define Verbal Sentence (الجملة الفعلية) in Arabic. Explain the structure of Verb (الفعل), Subject (الفاعل), and Object (المفعول به) with complete examples.\pts{12}

\medskip\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\medskip

\noindent\textbf{Question 2.} Conjugate the trilateral past tense verb (الفعل الماضي) \textbf{كَتَبَ} (to write) for all 14 personal pronouns in Arabic.\pts{12}

\medskip\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\medskip

\noindent\textbf{Question 3.} Conjugate the present/future tense verb (الفعل المضارع) \textbf{يَذْهَبُ} (to go) for all 14 personal pronouns.\pts{12}

\medskip\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\medskip

\noindent\textbf{Question 4.} Translate the following English sentences into Arabic:
\begin{parts}
  \item The student went to the university library yesterday.
  \item The doctor writes a prescription for the patient.
  \item We read a useful book in the classroom.
  \item She eats an apple in the morning.
\end{parts}\pts{12}

\medskip\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\medskip

\noindent\textbf{Question 5.} Explain the function and usage of Particles of Negation (أدوات النفي) such as \textbf{مَا}, \textbf{لاَ}, and \textbf{لَمْ} with suitable sentence examples.\pts{12}
