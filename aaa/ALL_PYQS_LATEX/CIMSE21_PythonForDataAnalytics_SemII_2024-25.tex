\noindent\textbf{Question 1} \textit{(Compulsory)} \\
Write the codes in SageMath for the following mathematical operations and then solve the corresponding question without using codes:
\begin{parts}
  \item To find the rank of a matrix and hence find the rank of the matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 8 & 16 \\ 1 & 2 & 4 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$.
  \item To find the adjoint of a matrix $A$ and hence find the adjoint of the matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 2 \\ 1 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$.
  \item To find the remainder when an integer $a$ is divided by a positive integer $b$ and hence find the remainder when $(2025)^3$ is divided by $47$.
  \item To solve a system of linear equations $2x + y = 6, 5x - y = 4$ in two variables and hence solve it manually.
  \item To plot the curve $y^2 = x + 2y$ in $\mathbb{R}^2$ and hence plot it.
  \item To find the dot product of two vectors in $\mathbb{R}^3$ and hence find the dot product of two vectors which represent the sides of an equilateral triangle of side length $1$.
  \item To plot a vector field in $\mathbb{R}^2$ and hence plot the vector field $V(x,y) = (-y, x)$ in the region $\{(x,y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 \mid x^2 + y^2 \le 1\}$.
\end{parts}

\medskip\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\medskip

\noindent\textbf{Question 2}
\begin{parts}
  \item Write a 3-dimensional Cartesian equation of a line in $xz$-plane, passing through origin and making equal angle with the two axes. Write the codes in SageMath to plot this line.
  \item What is RREF of a matrix? Write a SageMath program to find the RREF of the matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 2 & 3 & 4 \\ 4 & 5 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$. Compute manually to find its output.
\end{parts}

\medskip\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\medskip

\noindent\textbf{Question 3}
\begin{parts}
  \item State Lagrange's Mean Value Theorem and verify it for the function $f(x) = x^2 + 3x + 2$ on the interval $[-1, 1]$. Can you interpret it geometrically? Write a SageMath program to check the validity of your computation.
  \item Write a SageMath program to plot the tangent line to a circle of your choice at any of its points so that the colors of the circle and the tangent line appear differently.
\end{parts}

\medskip\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\medskip

\noindent\textbf{Question 4}
\begin{parts}
  \item Define eigenvalue and eigenvector of a square matrix $A$ and hence find them for the matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ 1 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$. Write codes in SageMath to find eigenvalues and eigenvectors of a square matrix $A$.
  \item Define diagonalizability of a square matrix. Determine whether the matrix $A$, as defined above, is diagonalizable. Write a SageMath program to check the diagonalizability of a square matrix.
\end{parts}

\medskip\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\medskip

\noindent\textbf{Question 5}
\begin{parts}
  \item Find the area of the region enclosed by the parabola $y^2 = 4x$ and the line $y = 2x$ and write the SageMath code to compute it.
  \item What will be the output of the following SageMath program:
  \[
  \text{\texttt{integrate(1/(1 + e\textasciicircum x), (x, 0, 2))}}
  \]
\end{parts}

\medskip\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\medskip

\noindent\textbf{Question 6}
\begin{parts}
  \item Find the differential equation of all circles which touch the line $y = x$ at the origin and hence by solving it find the equation of that circle which has intercept $1$ on positive $x$-axis. Also write a SageMath program to solve the differential equation.
  \item Find the differential equation of the curves passing through the origin given that the slope of the tangent to the curve at any point $(x,y)$ is equal to the sum of the coordinates of the point and hence write SageMath code to solve it.
\end{parts}

\medskip\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\medskip

\noindent\textbf{Question 7}
\begin{parts}
  \item Find the volume of the parallelepiped formed by vectors $(1,1,1)$, $(1,1,0)$ and $(2,3,4)$ in $\mathbb{R}^3$ as its adjacent edges. Also write the codes in SageMath to find the volume of the parallelepiped, whose three adjacent edges are given as vectors in $\mathbb{R}^3$.
  \item Write a SageMath program to plot a right circular cylinder with $z$-axis as its axis and radius $1$ in two different ways.
\end{parts}
