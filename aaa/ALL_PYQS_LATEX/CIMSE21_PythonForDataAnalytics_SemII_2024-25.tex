\noindent\textbf{Question 1} \textit{(Compulsory)} \\
Write the codes in SageMath for the following mathematical operations and then solve the corresponding question without using codes:
\begin{parts}
  \item To find the rank of a matrix and hence find the rank of $A = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 8 & 16 \\ 1 & 2 & 4 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$.\pts{2}
  \item To find the adjoint of a matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 4 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$.\pts{2}
  \item To find the remainder when an integer $a$ is divided by a positive integer $b$ and hence find the remainder when $(2025)^2$ is divided by 47.\pts{2}
  \item To solve a system of linear equations $2x + y = 6, 5x - y = 4$ in two variables and solve it manually.\pts{2}
  \item To plot the curve $y^2 = x + 2y$ in $\mathbb{R}^2$ and hence plot it.\pts{2}
  \item To find the dot product of two vectors in $\mathbb{R}^3$ and find the dot product of two vectors representing sides of an equilateral triangle of side length 1.\pts{2}
  \item To plot a vector field $V(x, y) = (-y, x)$ in the region $\{(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 \mid x^2 + y^2 \le 1\}$.\pts{2}
\end{parts}

\medskip\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\medskip

\noindent\textbf{Question 2}
\begin{parts}
  \item Write a 3-dimensional Cartesian equation of a line in $xz$-plane, passing through origin and making equal angle with the two axes. Write the codes in SageMath to plot this line.\pts{7}
  \item What is RREF of a matrix? Write a SageMath program to find the RREF of $A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 3 & 4 \\ 3 & 4 & 5 \\ 4 & 5 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$. Compute manually to find its output.\pts{7}
\end{parts}

\medskip\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\medskip

\noindent\textbf{Question 3}
\begin{parts}
  \item State Lagrange mean value theorem and verify it for $f(x) = x^2 + 3x + 2$ on $[-1, 1]$. Interpret geometrically and write SageMath code.\pts{7}
  \item Write a SageMath program to plot the tangent line to a circle of your choice at any point so that circle and line appear in different colors.\pts{7}
\end{parts}

\medskip\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\medskip

\noindent\textbf{Question 4}
\begin{parts}
  \item Define eigenvalue and eigenvector of a matrix. Write SageMath code to find eigenvalues and eigenvectors of $A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$.\pts{7}
  \item Explain diagonalizability of a matrix. Determine whether $A$ above is diagonalizable and write a SageMath program to test diagonalizability.\pts{7}
\end{parts}

\medskip\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\medskip

\noindent\textbf{Question 5}
\begin{parts}
  \item Find the area enclosed by $y^2 = 4x$ and $x = 2$, and write SageMath code to compute it.\pts{7}
  \item What will be the output of the SageMath code: \texttt{integrate((1/(1 + e\textasciicircum(sin(x)))), (x, 0, 2*pi))}?\pts{7}
\end{parts}

\medskip\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\medskip

\noindent\textbf{Question 6}
\begin{parts}
  \item Find the differential equation of circles touching $y = x$ at origin and find the circle with $x$-intercept 1. Write SageMath program.\pts{7}
  \item Find differential equation of curves through origin where slope equals sum of coordinates ($dy/dx = x + y$) and write SageMath code.\pts{7}
\end{parts}

\medskip\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\medskip

\noindent\textbf{Question 7}
\begin{parts}
  \item Find the volume of parallelopiped formed by $(1,1,1), (1,1,0), (2,3,4)$ in $\mathbb{R}^3$. Write SageMath code.\pts{7}
  \item Write SageMath program to plot a right circular cylinder with $z$-axis as axis and radius 1 in two different ways.\pts{7}
\end{parts}
