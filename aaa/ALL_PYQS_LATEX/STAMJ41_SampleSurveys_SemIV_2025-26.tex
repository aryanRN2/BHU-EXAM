\noindent\textbf{Question 1} \textit{(Compulsory)}
\begin{parts}
  \item Discuss consistency criterion of a good estimator. If $X_1, X_2, \dots, X_n$ are $n$ random observations taken from geometric distribution with parameter $\theta$, then show that $\bar{X}$ is consistent estimator of $\frac{1}{\theta}$. Also, obtain a consistent estimator of $e^{1/\theta}$.\pts{3.5}
  \item Explain point and interval estimation with proper examples. Write the confidence interval for the population mean when variance is known.\pts{3.5}
  \item Discuss sampling distribution of a statistic and standard error with its utility. A machine produces a large number of items of which 15\% are found to be defective. If a random sample of 200 items is taken from the population, then find the standard error of sampling distribution of proportion.\pts{3.5}
  \item Write difference between parametric and non-parametric tests. The cars that enter in a parking are classified either Foreign-made (\textbf{F}) or Indian-Made (\textbf{I}). To check that the car enter in the parking are in random order, a sample of first 40 cars that entered in the parking is taken and found the following sequence:
  \begin{center}
  \texttt{IIFFFIFFFFFIIFIIIFFIFFIFFFIFFIIFFFIFFII}
  \end{center}
  Obtain number of runs and expected value of number of runs in the above sequence?\pts{3.5}
\end{parts}

\noindent\textbf{Question 2}
\begin{parts}
  \item Define Efficiency. If $X_1, X_2, \dots, X_n$ is a random sample taken from a population having mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$, then show that the statistic $T' = \frac{1}{n+1}\sum_{i=1}^n X_i$ is biased but more efficient than sample mean for estimating the population mean.\pts{7}
  \item If $T_1$ and $T_2$ be the two unbiased estimators of $\gamma(\theta)$ with variances $\sigma_1^2$ and $\sigma_2^2$ and correlation coefficient $\rho$, what is the best linear unbiased combination of $T_1$ and $T_2$, and what is the variance of such a combination?\pts{7}
\end{parts}
