\noindent \textbf{Question 1.} Attempt \textit{any four} of the following: \pts{4 $\times$ 3.5 = 14}
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
  \item Write four differences between a battery and a fuel cell?
  \item Give the definitions of volumetric and gravimetric density. In a typical high pressure hydrogen storage cylinder, how do gravimetric hydrogen density change with increasing pressure?
  \item What are the objectives of National Green Hydrogen Mission?
  \item What is the meaning of net-zero emission?
  \item What is quantum efficiency? Describe the spectral response of a photovoltaics. Describe the mechanism of low efficiency at red and blue side.
  \item Name the major types of energy storage technologies and give example of each technology.
  \item Write down the challenges in developing a thermal energy storage plant.
  \item What is a flow battery? What are the drawbacks of Nickel-based batteries?
\end{enumerate}
