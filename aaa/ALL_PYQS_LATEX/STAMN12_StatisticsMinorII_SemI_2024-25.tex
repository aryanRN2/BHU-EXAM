\noindent\textbf{1)} Given below are the marks obtained in Mathematics by a batch of 84 students of a school:

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
43 & 34 & 43 & 32 & 87 & 35 & 71 & 65 & 12 & 52 & 19 & 48 & 17 & 24 \\ \hline
52 & 65 & 40 & 54 & 62 & 45 & 02 & 13 & 18 & 49 & 57 & 21 & 64 & 71 \\ \hline
45 & 81 & 52 & 40 & 35 & 78 & 43 & 45 & 44 & 55 & 79 & 37 & 19 & 14 \\ \hline
37 & 71 & 51 & 35 & 27 & 74 & 22 & 08 & 22 & 15 & 20 & 23 & 35 & 27 \\ \hline
17 & 21 & 52 & 48 & 09 & 22 & 12 & 12 & 85 & 40 & 39 & 30 & 42 & 08 \\ \hline
66 & 74 & 19 & 04 & 30 & 06 & 19 & 31 & 25 & 38 & 32 & 51 & 68 & 43 \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{parts}
  \item Summarize the data in a grouped frequency distribution using the formula given by H. A. Sturges.
  \item Find the proportion of students scoring (i) less than 30 marks and (ii) more than 75 marks.
  \item Construct a frequency histogram for the data and comment on the shape of the distribution.
  \item Draw the ogives and hence find the mark below which 50\% of the students scored.
\end{parts}
