\section*{SECTION -- A}
\noindent \textit{Note: Answer the following questions in about 400 words each:}\pts{3 $\times$ 12 = 36}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.},leftmargin=*,itemsep=12pt]

  \item Attempt a definition of Essay. In what ways do you find it different from the other genres of Literature?\pts{12}

  \begin{center}\textbf{OR}\end{center}

  How can you distinguish different kinds of Essays on the basis of style?\pts{12}

  \item What are Bacon's main arguments in ``Of Studies''?\pts{12}

  \begin{center}\textbf{OR}\end{center}

  Critically analyze Orwell's ``The Sporting Spirit''.\pts{12}

  \item Give your opinion on the narrator's experience at the Bank in ``My Financial Career'' by Stephen Leacock. Do you find it a common experience?\pts{12}

  \begin{center}\textbf{OR}\end{center}

  What vision of nation and humanity does Nehru express in his essay ``Opportunity for Youth''? Discuss.\pts{12}

\end{enumerate}

\bigskip

\section*{SECTION -- B}
\noindent \textit{Note: Medium answer type questions to be answered in about 150 words each:}\pts{4 $\times$ 6 = 24}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.},leftmargin=*,start=4,itemsep=10pt]

  \item What pieces of advice do you get from Sir Andrew Freeport to become rich and industrious?\pts{6}

  \begin{center}\textbf{OR}\end{center}

  Write a short note on `art-snobs' as described by Huxley in ``Selected Snobberies''.\pts{6}

  \item What are the memorable features of John Lamb's personality as reminisced nostalgically by Charles Lamb?\pts{6}

  \begin{center}\textbf{OR}\end{center}

  Why does Swami Vivekananda exhort to ``work with freedom, inspired by love!'' and not by slavery?\pts{6}

  \item Why should ``training in leadership'' be an important part of education for new India according to C. Rajagopalachari?\pts{6}

  \begin{center}\textbf{OR}\end{center}

  Describe any incident from ``Dream-Children: A Reverie'' where Lamb subtly blends humor and pathos.\pts{6}

  \item How are psychological and sociological implications of sports different in urban and rural settings according to Orwell?\pts{6}

  \begin{center}\textbf{OR}\end{center}

  Why is the bank manager annoyed with the narrator in ``My Financial Career''?\pts{6}

\end{enumerate}
