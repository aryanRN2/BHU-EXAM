\noindent\textbf{Question 2.} Discuss the methods and techniques of resource appraisal used in land resource.\\

\noindent\textbf{Question 3.} Explain the concept of resource conservation and discuss the methods adopted for conserving water resources in India.\\

\medskip\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}\medskip

\noindent\textbf{Question 4.} Describe the importance of human resource development and highlight the problems of developing countries.\\

\noindent\textbf{Question 5.} Mention the main objectives of environmental planning and discuss the strategies adopted in India.\\
