\noindent   \textbf{Question 1.} Answer the following parts:

\begin{parts}
  \item State whether the following statements are True or False. Justify your answer in 2--3 sentences each:\pts{$5  \times 2 = 10$}
  
\begin{parts}
    \item Karan Swiss is developed at the National Dairy Research Institute of India (NDRI), Karnal by crossing of Brown Swiss and Sindhi breeds of cow.
    \item Drone bees are unable to guard the hive against intruders.
    \item The GloFish is a genetically modified fish produced by integrating the Green Fluorescent Protein (GFP) gene from firefly into the embryo of Zebrafish (   \textit{Danio rerio}).
    \item   \textit{Streptococcus thermophilus} is used as a starter in curd, yogurt and cheese.
    \item Lac, the commercial resin of animal origin, is secreted only by the larvae of   \textit{Laccifer lacca}.
  \end{parts}

  \item Define the following:\pts{$6  \times 1 = 6$}
  
\begin{parts}
    \item Mariculture
    \item Koinobiont parasitoid
    \item Propolis
    \item Broiler
    \item Low-temperature-longer-time pasteurization
    \item Stifling.
  \end{parts}

  \item Differentiate between the following:\pts{$2  \times 2 = 4$}
  
\begin{parts}
    \item Finfish and Shellfish
    \item Snake venom and Bee venom.
  \end{parts}
\end{parts}
