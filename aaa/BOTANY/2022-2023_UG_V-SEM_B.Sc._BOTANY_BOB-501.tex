\medskip\noindent\textbf{Question 1.} Briefly answer the following in not more than 50 words each:\pts{$10 \times 1 = 10$}
\begin{parts}
  \item Define coenobium.
  \item Differentiate between zoospore and hypnospore.
  \item What is the function of the Retort cell and its location?
  \item Differentiate between isomorphic and heteromorphic alternation of generations.
  \item What do you mean by perfect state of the fungi?
  \item Name one fungus used in the pharmaceutical industry.
  \item What is diplanetism? In which group of fungi is it observed?
  \item Differentiate between ectophloic siphonostele and amphiphloic siphonostele.
  \item Differentiate between true and false indusium.
  \item Write the systematic position of \textit{Psilotum}.
\end{parts}
