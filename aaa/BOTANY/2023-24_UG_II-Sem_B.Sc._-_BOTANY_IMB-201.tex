\noindent   \textbf{Question 1.} Answer the following (not more than 50 words each):\pts{$10  \times 1 = 10$}

\begin{parts}
  \item What is the recognition sequence, restriction site and the fragments produced by the restriction enzyme KpnI?
  \item Comment on BOD and COD.
  \item Give an example of a polyextremophile.
  \item What are inclusion bodies?
  \item Name a plant that degrades pesticides.
  \item Give an example of a halophile.
  \item What is associative mutualism? Provide an example.
  \item What is the organic loading rate?
  \item Name the micro-organism responsible for causing bloody diarrhoea in infants.
  \item Name the channel involved in the regulation of glycerol content of a cell.
\end{parts}
