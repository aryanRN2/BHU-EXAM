Also enumerate the different kinds of decision that managers need to take.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Question 2.} What do you mean by delegation of authority? What are the various steps involved in
- delegation of authority? Is it essential to delegate authority in any organization?

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Question 3.} Environmental-Scanning is necessary for strategy formulation. In the light of the
above statement state the relevance of environmental scanning. Also write a brief note about any four
techniques of environmental scanning.
\centerline{\textbf{OR}}

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Question 4.} Explain the meaning of strategic choice and strategic implementation. What are the
steps involved in strategy implementation? How is strategic implementation relevant in the present
corporate world?

traditional management?

one?

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Question 4.} What are the different types of incentives that a manager can make use of to motivate
his subordinates?

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Question 5.} What do you understand by ‘Strategy’ in business? Explain any two types of strategy.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Question 6.} ‘Vision’ provides a roadmap to company’s future. How is it different from the

performing ‘Company's situation/position analysis’?

implementation?
