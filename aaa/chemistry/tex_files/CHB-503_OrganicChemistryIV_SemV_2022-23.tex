\noindent\textbf{Question 1.} Answer all parts of the following: \pts{5 $\times$ 2 = 10}
\begin{parts}
  \item Why does Michler's ketone not undergo photoreduction to pinacol?
  \item Differentiate between singlet and triplet carbenes in terms of spin state and geometry.
  \item Write the product when cyclohexanone is reacted with diazomethane.
  \item Predict the product: $(E)$-stilbene + singlet carbene ($\text{:CH}_2$) $\to ?$
  \item Why does benzidine rearrangement occur intramolecularly?
\end{parts}

\noindent\textbf{Question 2.}
\begin{parts}
  \item Discuss the stereochemical conformations of $1,2$-dimethylcyclohexane and comment on their relative stabilities. \pts{7}
  \item Discuss Bayer's strain theory and its limitations for higher cycloalkanes. \pts{7}
\end{parts}

\medskip\rule{\linewidth}{0.2pt}

\noindent\textbf{Question 3.}
\begin{parts}
  \item Describe the intermolecular and intramolecular insertion reactions of carbenes. \pts{6}
  \item Predict the correct stability order of the following carbocations, with reasons: benzyl cation, cyclopropylmethyl cation, and isopropyl cation. \pts{4}
  \item Explain the concept of Kinetic Isotope Effect (KIE) and how it is useful for predicting reaction mechanisms. \pts{4}
\end{parts}
